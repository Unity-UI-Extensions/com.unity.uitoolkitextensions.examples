## Installing the Examples

The examples are included with the package as the **UI Toolkit Extensions Samples** package sample. Once the package is installed, import them from the Unity Package Manager:

***Package Manager -> Unity UI Toolkit Extensions -> Samples -> Import***

## Examples

- **Action Menu** — Anchored overflow menus using DropDownMenuControl: per-row ··· triggers and a centered card menu.
- **ScrollSnap + PageDotIndicator** — Horizontal paging with dot indicator and a ComingSoonMessage page.
- **Registration Form** — Full form using PillInputField, RoundedInputField, PillButton, PillSelector, and shake validation.
- **Step Wizard** — Multi-step flow using QuadrantStepper and StepProgressBar.
- **Content Explorer** — LoadingIcon reveal with CollapsibleSection and IconLabelButton items.
- **Profile Editor** — CircularImageButton, GrayscaleImage, ToggleButton, and ColorToggleGroup.
- **Toast Notifications** — Swipe-to-dismiss toast stack using ToastSwipeDismissManipulator.
- **Dropdown Phone Entry** — Phone-number entry pairing a country-code DropDownControl with PillInputFields.
- **Image Crop Overlay** — Pan / pinch-zoom crop flow using ImageCropOverlayControl and CircularImageButton.
- **Notification List** — Elastic notification feed using ElasticListView, NotificationBadge, and PillButton.
- **Screen Header** — Top app-bar demo wiring ScreenHeader's title and action events.
- **Scroll Snap (Split Views)** — The same ScrollSnap built three ways — C#, UXML, and a split layout.
- **Social Links** — Editable social-links section using SocialLinkContainer and PillButton.
- **Segmented Control** - QuadrantStepper's general-purpose name: equal and content sizing, icons, a disabled segment and keyboard navigation.
- **Bottom Sheet** - Peek, half and full detents with drag and fling, a modal scrim, a light surface and a docked tablet layout.
- **Modal Dialog** - Confirm, destructive, input and choice dialogs awaited through ModalDialog.Show, with focus trap, scrim and back handling.
- **Pill Button Variants** - Token-styled primary, ghost and warn PillButtons at full and compact height, with the disabled state.
- **Toast Host** - Queued toasts with tones, one action, undo, swipe and tap to dismiss, anchored top or bottom.
- **Status Chip** - Persistent statuses keyed by id through StatusChipHost: busy, ok, warn and fail, updated in place and resolved.
- **Switch** - Labelled on/off switches as BaseField<bool>, with reduce motion and the disabled state.
- **Settings Row** - Settings rows with chevron, value, switch and custom trailing slots on light and deep surfaces.
- **Scrolling Tabs** - Dynamic categories as scrolling tabs or chips, with counts, a search tab, an animated indicator and edge fades.
- **Ornament Bar** - A floating action bar with a primary item that anchors bottom centre in portrait and to the right edge in landscape.
- **Search Field** - Debounced search with a result count, submit and clear, loading skeletons and an empty state.
- **Skeleton Tile** - Tile, row, text and circle loading placeholders that hold still with reduce motion, and an EmptyState.
- **Progress Ring** - Determinate rings with a percentage, a count and the complete state, drawn in the token colours.
- **Status List** - An entry sequence with active, done and failed steps and an overall progress bar.
- **Coaching Overlay** - The four AR coaching cues with plain-language text, reduce motion and tap to dismiss.
- **Collapsible Section Card** - A permissions-style accordion: CollapsibleSection cards with icon tiles, subtitles, status badges and a static mode.
- **Content Grid** - A virtualised grid of 200 tiles with selection modes, long-press drag-out, and loading and empty states.
- **Section List** - A list-first browser: expandable sections with counts and rows, with single-open mode.
- **Side Drawer** - An options drawer with edge swipe, scrim, sections, right anchor and the permanent wide-layout variant.
- **Empty State** - Empty states with an icon and one action, text only, and on a light card.
- **Themed Variants** - Token-styled variants of existing controls: ScreenHeader Compact and RoundedInputField Themed on light and deep grounds.
- **World Anchored Label** - Labels that follow cubes through the camera, with tones, distance, safe-area clamping and hiding.
- **Tool Rail** - An action rail for a selected object and a mode rail with one active tool, with the gesture hint.
- **Gesture Hint** - The first-use gesture hint in touch and mouse wording, shown once and dismissed by a gesture or a tap.
- **Audio Guide Player** - Guide and video controls driven by a simulated source: scrub, skip, loop, rate, replay and transcript.
- **Entry Gate** - Four launch screens from one gate: safety notice, permission failure, experience entry with loading steps, and could-not-load.
- **Caption Bar** - Captions with a speaker column, clamped to three lines, cross-fading between cues, with text scale and reduce motion.
- **Prompt Choices** - Numbered character prompts with used prompts and number keys, an avatar header and the hold-to-talk row.
- **Speech Input Panel** - Speech input: listening with a level meter, partial and final transcripts, match, no match and unavailable.
- **Character Extras** - AvatarPortrait sizes, LinearProgress, an AchievementRow that fills, and the ShutterButton.
- **Context Pill** - the context pill at the top left of AR screens: a logo, a title and an eyebrow, and the dimmed state.
- **Scan Frame** - corner brackets for the scanner: tones, the searching pulse, and following a tracked marker.
- **QRCode View** - a QR code for a link, drawn without a barcode library, and saved as a PNG.
- **Hint Image Card** - a picture of what to face with a line of guidance and the FAR / NEAR / FOUND strip.
- **Wayfinder Card** - directions to the next stop: a turning arrow, the distance, and the arrived state.
